Built From the Floor, Not a Textbook

We don't just talk about customer support — we've lived it. Our approach is shaped by years of hands-on operational experience, which is why we deliver teams and solutions that actually perform.

Operational Experience That Drives Results

Maljian CX was founded by Khatchig Maljian, who started in customer support at 18 years old — headset on, handling real customers with real problems. No theory. No textbook. Just the floor.

Over six years, he worked across frontline support, mentoring, and Senior Trainer roles — building QA frameworks, designing onboarding systems, writing SOPs, and managing performance improvement inside live operations. He has seen firsthand what slows teams down, what confuses employees, and what actually drives measurable improvement.

In one recent engagement, his training and operational overhaul reduced a team's error rate from 50% to an average of 4% — not through theory, but by rebuilding training methods, simplifying workflows, and creating clearer internal documentation.

That depth of operational experience is what sets Maljian CX apart. When we build and manage a support team for your business, or audit your existing operation, we're applying methods proven in real customer-facing environments — not recycled consulting frameworks.

Why We Built Maljian CX

Across different companies, we kept seeing the same pattern: businesses invest heavily in products, marketing, and growth — while the operational side of customer experience gets neglected. Training is inconsistent. Workflows are unclear. Internal communication breaks down. The result? Mistakes, frustrated customers, employee burnout, and unnecessary costs.

At the same time, we saw businesses spending enormous amounts of money and management energy on building and maintaining in-house support teams — recruiting, hiring, onboarding, training, managing, and constantly replacing agents. Especially for companies needing bilingual English and Arabic support, the challenge was even harder and more expensive.

Maljian CX was built to solve both problems. For businesses that want to offload the entire support function, we provide fully managed, professional teams — English and Arabic-speaking — from our base in Armenia, at a cost structure that makes financial sense. For businesses that already have teams but aren't seeing the performance they need, we provide deep operational audits and tailored improvement plans.

Who We Work With

We partner with businesses that depend on consistent, high-quality customer communication — especially companies that are scaling and need support operations that can scale with them, without becoming a drain on leadership time and resources.

Our clients typically share one of these situations:

  • They're spending too much time and money managing support instead of growing their business
  • They need bilingual support (English and Arabic) but can't find or afford quality hires locally
  • Their existing team isn't performing, and they need to identify what's broken and how to fix it
  • They're scaling quickly and need a support function that can grow without operational chaos

The Armenia Advantage

We operate from Armenia for a reason. The country has a deep pool of educated, English-fluent, and Arabic-speaking professionals — with a cost structure that allows us to deliver exceptional support teams at a fraction of what equivalent hires cost in the US or Europe.

This isn't about cutting corners. It's about smart economics. Our agents receive structured training, ongoing quality assurance, and professional performance management — the same standards you'd expect from an in-house team. The difference is you get it without the recruitment burden, the HR overhead, or the management drain.

What We Believe

Strong support teams aren't built through scripts and memorization. They're built through understanding, structure, and practical preparation for real customer interactions under real operational pressure.

Every business has different workflows. Every team has different challenges. There is no universal template. What matters is identifying the specific operational problems causing friction — and building systems that make both employees and customer interactions more effective.

That's how we approach every managed team we run and every audit we conduct. Practical. Tailored. Built for the real world.
